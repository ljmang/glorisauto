# English Copy Style Guide (Working Draft)

Use this as the working baseline for English UI and CMS copy. Product names, specifications, and regulated or comparative claims still require confirmation from the product or compliance owner.

## Voice and mechanics

- Use clear, concise international B2B English. The English locale currently uses `en-US`, so prefer US spelling (for example, “color” and “center”).
- Use sentence case for body copy, form labels, and buttons. Keep official product names and registered marks in their approved form.
- Prefer concrete benefits and plain verbs over stacked promotional adjectives such as “elite,” “premium,” and “highly competitive” unless the claim is approved and substantiated.
- Keep one idea per sentence. Remove repeated headings, editorial notes, and explanations that are only useful to an internal translator.
- In English copy, use the approved Romanized brand name “Parrot® Gloris.” Retain Chinese characters only when the official Chinese legal/brand name is specifically required; do not add parenthetical translator notes to customer-facing copy.

## Working glossary

| Preferred wording | Avoid / review | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sandpaper | sand paper | One word for the general product family; use a more specific name such as “film sanding disc” when that is the actual product. |
| automotive refinishing | automotive refinish (as a noun phrase) | “Refinish” can remain in established product-category names such as “automotive refinish products.” |
| body shop | bodyshop | Use two words in general copy. |
| distributor / distribution partner | dealer (when referring to the B2B channel) | Use “dealer” only if it identifies a distinct, formally defined channel. |
| wet/dry sanding | wet dry sanding | Preserve product names if the approved SKU name uses a different form. |
| Technical Resources | Downloads (for the product-detail resource section) | Keep the section heading and its navigation label consistent. |

## Content checks before publication

- Verify product size, pack contents, mixing ratios, drying times, coverage, MOQ, and units against the approved technical data sheet. Never infer a correction from neighboring products.
- Confirm evidence and scope for absolute or comparative claims, including “100%,” “leading,” “comparable,” and promised margin or territory protection.
- Check English pages for untranslated Chinese text, full-width Chinese punctuation, duplicated sentences, malformed units/decimals, and inconsistent product-family naming.
- Check every form label and CTA in context, including mobile layouts and validation/error states.
